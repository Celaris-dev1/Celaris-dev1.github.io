Prosper legal

Plain-language terms for the Prosper software and licence, how we handle personal data, and how the launch offer and refunds work. Questions: prosper@omucloud.co.

Terms of use

1. What you get

Prosper is software you run on your own computer. A licence (activated with a PIN) lets you use it on the number of machines its plan includes. A licence is personal to the buyer, is not transferable, and the PIN must not be shared beyond its seat count. An unactivated copy does nothing until it is activated.

2. Who owns what you make

Games Prosper generates for you are yours. To the extent the law gives anyone rights in a generated game, Prolu claims none and assigns whatever rights it has to you. Prosper itself, its templates, add-ons and packs stay ours; your licence lets you ship games built from them, but you may not resell or give away the templates, add-ons or packs on their own, or use them to build a competing game maker.

Games are partly written by the AI models you choose and pay for. The law on ownership of AI-generated material differs by country and is unsettled. We do not promise that model output is free of third-party rights, or that you can register copyright in it.

3. Third-party assets

Games can include third-party art, sound and models under their own licences (for example CC0 and CC-BY). Each build lists them in ATTRIBUTIONS.md next to the game. You must ship that file, or an equivalent credits screen, with the game. You are responsible for any asset you add yourself.

4. What you must not do

Use Prosper to make or distribute unlawful content, content that infringes others' rights, or malware; remove or bypass licence checks; resell or share PINs beyond your seats; or attack the activation service.

5. Payment, renewal and expiry

A paid term is 30 days (monthly) or 365 days (yearly, priced at ten months). Payment is by card (Paystack) or by bank transfer in US dollars. Nothing renews automatically: you renew by paying again with your PIN. After a term ends there is a 3-day grace period, then Prosper stops building until you renew. Games you already made keep working and remain yours.

6. No warranty, limited liability

Prosper v0.1.0 is functional but not polished, and is provided as it is, without warranty. Generated games can contain bugs; test them before you ship. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the amount you paid for the licence in the 12 months before the claim. Nigerian law governs, except where your own consumer law says otherwise.

Privacy

Prosper is run by Prolu, Nigeria. We are the controller of the data below, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. If you live elsewhere you may also have rights under your own law (for example the GDPR).

The short version. Prosper runs on your machine. Your projects, generated games and AI provider keys stay on it. We receive only what is needed to sell, activate and support your licence.

What we collect and why

  • Email, payment status and order details, from checkout. Card payments are handled by Paystack; we never see card numbers. Used to take payment, issue your PIN and support you.
  • PIN, a one-way machine fingerprint and activation times, sent by Prosper to our activation server, to enforce seats and keep your licence valid.
  • Bank-transfer details (name and reference on the transfer), to match your payment.
  • Download requests (file, day and IP address), to limit abuse.
  • Support emails you send us.

What we do not collect

Your game prompts, projects, generated code and AI provider keys are not sent to us. Prosper sends no usage analytics to us. Prompts and code go from your machine to the AI providers you choose, under their terms, not ours. We do not collect build data for training.

Who receives data

Paystack (card payments), our hosting providers (Cloudflare and our server host) and our email provider (Gmail). We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Licence and payment records for as long as tax and accounting law requires; download logs for about 90 days; support email for up to 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, subject to our duty to keep tax records. Email prosper@omucloud.co. You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or to your own data protection authority. Prosper is not for children under 16.

Refunds and the launch offer

Launch offer

The first 100 people to claim the launch code get a free licence for one machine, valid for 60 days from the moment they claim it. The code has no date; it stops working when the 100th person has used it. One claim per email. No payment details are taken and nothing is charged afterwards: when your 60 days (plus the 3-day grace) end, the licence stops unless you buy a paid term. Spots are first come, first served and are not reserved.

Refunds

If you have not activated your PIN, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying. Once a PIN has been activated, payments are not refundable, except where the law of your country gives you a right to one. Email prosper@omucloud.co from the address you bought with. A failed or disputed payment pauses the licence until it is fixed.

Abuse

We may revoke a licence for sharing PINs, using more seats than allowed, reselling, or attacking the service, and will say why where we can.
